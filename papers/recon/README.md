# Initial public literature and reference acquisition

The [expanded research map](../survey/README.md) indexes the broader study.
This directory retains [initial primary reading](literature.md),
[public reference provenance](external-manifest.md), and
[public acquisition gaps](literature-shopping.md).

Private sources may inform our reasoning, but their identities, descriptions,
paths, inventories, revisions and source-specific findings do not belong in this
repository. Private acquisitions and identifying notes are kept outside it.

Deleuze captures every event reaching its gate, extremely quickly, at a
reliability target funded by the deployment. Full/failing-gate arrivals and
retries remain in capture accounting. Native Rust and polyglot entry paths must
state their ownership, completion and failure semantics. No architecture or
dependency has been selected.
